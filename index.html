What we do

We connect. You decide.

U-Do is a directory with a phone book attached. We are not a contractor, an agency or an employer.

We do

  • ✓List workers by trade and area so you find the right person fast.
  • ✓Show you a verified phone number to call or WhatsApp.
  • ✓Check every number by OTP and keep a profile photo on file.
  • ✓Suspend or ban accounts that break the rules.

We don't

  • –Set prices, hold payments or take a cut of the worker's wage.
  • –Employ, supervise or guarantee any worker or customer.
  • –Take responsibility for the quality, safety or outcome of the job.
  • –Ask for your Emirates ID or passport.

Safety

Real people, real numbers

OTP on every phone

Customers and workers confirm their mobile with a one-time code before anyone can see them.

Photo on every profile

You see who you are calling, and workers see who is calling them. Photos are only used for identification.

No ID documents

We never collect Emirates ID or passport copies. Less data held means less to leak.

Our responsibility, stated plainly

U-Do only shares contact details between customers and workers. Any agreement, payment, work quality, damage or dispute is between the customer and the worker. U-Do is not a party to the job and accepts no responsibility for it.
